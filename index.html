CONTROLS & COMBAT GUIDE

⚔️ SWORD MASTERY

  • Melee Attack: Swing combo with horizontal reach. Hitbox deals damage and knockback.
  • Dash Strike: Attack while dashing to lunge forward with invulnerability frames.
  • Parry Shield: Raise your guard at the precise moment to deflect arrows back at opponents and stun melee attackers!
  • Down-Thrust: Attack in mid-air while holding Down to pogo off foes and bounce high!

🏹 BOW & ARROW TACTICS

  • Quick Lob: Tap attack to fire a quick arc arrow. Great for close suppressive fire.
  • Charged Piercer: Hold attack to draw the bowstring. Releases a flat, hyper-velocity piercing arrow!
  • Quiver & Retrieval: Arrows stick into walls and platforms. Walk over them to restock your quiver.
  • Arrow Clash: Two opposing arrows colliding in mid-air deflect each other!
